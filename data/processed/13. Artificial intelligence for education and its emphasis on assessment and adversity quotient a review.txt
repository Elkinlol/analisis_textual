Abstract
Purpose – The purpose of this study was to analyze importance of artificial intelligence (AI) in education and
its emphasis on assessment and adversity quotient (AQ).
Design/methodology/approach – The study utilizes a systematic literature review of over 141 journal
papers and psychometric tests to evaluate AQ. Thematic analysis of quantitative and qualitative studies
explores domains of AI in education.
Findings – Results suggest that assessing the AQ of students with the help of AI techniques is necessary.
Education is a vital tool to develop and improve natural intelligence, and this survey presents the discourse use
of AI techniques and behavioral strategies in the education sector of the recent era. The study proposes a
conceptual framework of AQ with the help of assessment style for higher education undergraduates.
Originality/value – Research on AQ evaluation in the Indian context is still emerging, presenting a potential
avenue for future research. Investigating the relationship between AQ and academic performance among
Indian students is a crucial area of research. This can provide insights into the role of AQ in academic
motivation, persistence and success in different academic disciplines and levels of education. AQ evaluation
offers valuable insights into how individuals deal with and overcome challenges. The findings of this study
have implications for higher education institutions to prepare for future challenges and better equip students
with necessary skills for success. The papers reviewed related to AI for education opens research opportunities
in the field of psychometrics, educational assessment and the evaluation of AQ.

RQ1.
How AI benefits education domain?

AI plays a significant role (Lee et al., 2017) in the creation of knowledge sources,
knowledge material, online and virtual classes, assessment, administration, feedback
personalized learning, adaptive systems used in education. Section of AI techniques
discusses the literature, that Machine learning classified into four types (Matore and
Khairani, 2016) and each type of learning is used depending on the application (Sharma
et al., 2019; Chassignol et al., 2018; Shuguang et al., 2021). We have studied popular
techniques such as ML, DM (Namoun and Alshanqiti, 2021), NN , 2014; Halachev, 2013),
NLP (A. Mondal et al., 2018; Deng and Liu, 2018), GA (Wang, 2022; Julia et al., 2020) and

and Saafan, 2021; Deng and Liu, 2018; H€ohle and Klement, 2012) show, though every
technique has its own advantages and disadvantages, they are interrelated or there is a
thin boundary where the techniques use mixed methodology. ML is related with NN and
deep learning (Martin et al., 2014). Some of the studies (Srinivasan, 2022; Zhang et al., 2022;
Widodo et al., 2022) focus on intelligent and smart systems (Sarker, 2022). Few of the
studies (Luckin et al., 2017; Zhu et al., 2020; Sharma, 2022) focus on assessment-based
techniques used in education. Above investigations proved, that there has been
expansion in the AI techniques which can be used for specific educational purpose. At
the beginning of the lockdown due to COVID-19 pandemic, rise in technology usage seen,
i.e. for online coaching (through Zoom, Google Meet) and remote data storage (Google
Drive, Classroom). Significantly less work has been done in AI with teaching-learning to
construct full proof systems.

8. Limitations
The education sector is highly diverse worldwide, whereas the focus of the present study is
Indian higher education. The research specific literature investigation is extensively done
from 2013 to 2023. Use of AI techniques in education and that is for evaluating AQ is an
evolving area. It is very difficult to map multiple domains with single literature review,
i.e. Education, AI, Assessments (Psychometric þ Academics). Up-to-the-minute literary
works were done in AI-based AQ evaluation for higher education students. Since it is an
interdisciplinary emerging work, very less work is done in this area and therefore it is not
possible to compare AI techniques based on AQ.

field of psychometrics, educational assessment and AQ evaluation. This study highlights the
potential for evaluating AQ for higher education students. However, research on AQ
