With the rapid development of artiﬁcial intelligence (AI) technologies and a continuously growing interest in their
application in educational contexts, there has been signiﬁcant growth in the scientiﬁc literature in relation to the
application of AI in education (AIEd). This study aims to present multiple perspectives on the development of
AIEd in terms of relevant grants, conferences, journals, software tools, article trends, top issues, institutions, and
researchers to provide an overview of AIEd for its further development and implementation. With this study, we
contribute to the research ﬁeld by enabling educators and scholars to understand the status and development of
relevant grants and publications concerning AIEd. Also, ﬁndings concerning active actors can help educators and
scholars identify the active researchers and institutions in the research on AIEd. Furthermore, researchers and
educators are able to identify relevant journals and be more aware of major issues in AIEd studies. In addition, we
also highlight the signiﬁcance and necessity of the launch of the new Elsevier journal AIEd-related journal named
Computers & Education: Artiﬁcial Intelligence.

1. Introduction

Intrinsically, the future of education is, to a large degree, related to
the advances in novel intelligent technologies and the rapid development
of computational capacities. Hence, the educational community is facing
the opportunity and challenge brought by the continuously developing
artiﬁcial intelligence (AI) technologies, which can potentially and
fundamentally change the structure, operation, and governance of
educational institutes (Popenici & Kerr, 2017).

Technologies have a signiﬁcant impact on today’s society and human
beings. They have greatly changed the way we think, feel, act, and how
we communicate and interact with each other. Meanwhile, technological
advancements are rapidly and tremendously changing the ways in which
we learn and teach. Consequently, the landscape of education is gradu-
ally changing.

The work in AI is dedicated to addressing cognitive problems that are
commonly related to human intelligence. The educational sector is un-
deniably signiﬁcantly inﬂuenced by AI. AI applications in education
(AIEd) are widely used by learners and educators nowadays and involve
various tools and applications, for example, intelligent tutoring systems,
teaching robots, and adaptive learning systems. AIEd supports learning
occurring in both traditional classes and workplaces by combining AI and
various learning sciences, for example, education, psychology, linguis-
tics, and neuroscience, aiming at stimulating and advancing the devel-
opment of AI-driven educational applications featuring ﬂexibility,
personalization, and effectiveness(Luckin, Holmes, Grifﬁths, & Forcier,
2016).

Especially in recent years, with the advance and prevalence of
interactive technologies, their applications within and outside of classes
are steadily gaining in popularity. Such technologies aim to adapt to
learners’ diverse needs and situations, as well as to enhance competi-
tiveness in the global education market. Students utilize tablets rather
than copybooks, and teachers use various educational tools and appli-
cations such as Google Classroom and Moodle. Also, a great number of
Massive Open Online Course (MOOC) platforms like Class Central, EdX,
and Coursera are increasingly available for online learning. The
increasing number of active MOOC users indicates the wide acceptance
and appreciation of digital and distance learning technologies. Addi-
tionally, there are MOOCs that even provide learners with opportunities

To present a multi-perspective overview of the development of AIEd,
this study examines relevant grants, conferences, journals, software tools,
article trends, top issues, institutions, and researchers. Such an investi-
gation is important for the following reasons. First, scholars and their
projects largely depend on funding as the main research driver, and every
year considerable ﬁnancial resources are invested in facilitating scientiﬁc
activities. The trend analysis of funding in a particular ﬁeld is essential in
terms of examining whether the ﬁeld is of great importance and research
interest, and has great potential to make advances. Such analysis is also
helpful for decision-makers in the determination of effective funding
policies in (re)allocating resources to the most promising research ﬁelds
and projects (Ebadi & Schiffauerova, 2016). Second, analysis of trends in
scientiﬁc publications in a particular ﬁeld can reﬂect the evolution of
production and interest, as well as global scientiﬁc progress and devel-
opment tendency in the ﬁeld (Loonen et al., 2007; Ye et al., 2018).
Generally, the growth in the number of publications usually reﬂects an
expansion of the global science system concerning the ﬁeld (Aksnes et al.,
2016). Third, analysis of conferences and journals helps researchers and
students identify relevant publication sources with an international scope
(Song, Chen, Hao, Liu, & Lan, 2019). Fourth, the analysis of institutions
and researchers helps scholars identify major contributors to the ﬁeld
with the most potential to develop and share research (Mair & Rei-
schauer, 2017). Furthermore, analysis of research issues helps identify
the foci of research-based publications in a particular ﬁeld in the past,
current, and future (Snyder, 2011). In addition, a survey of relevant
software and products provides a basic understanding of the market size
concerning a particular ﬁeld, as well as what types of products have been
commonly invested in and what can be further improved. Accordingly,
there are six research questions we aim to answer:

We collected data from the Web of Science (WoS) using the advanced
search query provided in Table 1. We chose the WoS for the following
reasons. First, WoS is the most popularly used database for bibliometric
studies (Roemer & Borchardt, 2015). Second, the published studies in the
WoS were generally subjected to the meticulous and peer-review process,
ensuring high-quality publications. Additionally, in comparison to other
databases, the bibliographic data collected from the WoS were more
consistent and normative in expression. The search terms were deter-
mined by referring to previous studies (e.g., Heradio et al., 2016;
Martí-Parre~no et al., 2016; Tran et al., 2019, 2019a). The search was
conducted on October 3, 2020. A total of 9560 records were collected.
We analyzed the AIEd publication and citation trends and the major
keywords being used, as well as the major publication sources, in-
stitutions, and countries/regions to understand the research status of
AIEd research (e.g., Chen & Xie, 2020, Chen et al., 2018).

RQ1: What was the trend of grants in relation to AIEd?
RQ2: What were the major conferences and journals related to AIEd?
RQ3: What were the major software tools concerning AIEd?
RQ4: What was the trend of AIEd publications?
RQ5: What were the top frequently used keywords in AIEd
publications?
RQ6: What were the active institutions and researchers in the ﬁeld of
AIEd?

To identify the active researchers in the ﬁeld of AIEd, we used cita-
tions provided by Google Scholar. Speciﬁcally, we included authors with
a research interest in AIEd who had more than 1000 citations as of
August 3, 2020.

To identify the top frequently adopted keywords in the 9560 AIEd
publications, we collected the author-deﬁned keywords as well as the
keywords provided by the WoS (i.e., WoS Keywords Plus) for each
publication and calculated their frequencies to determine those that were
most commonly used.

We additionally provided an overview of some relevant journals,
conferences, and software tools related to AIEd.

3. Results

This study contributes to the research ﬁeld in several aspects. First, it
enables educators and scholars to understand the status and development
of relevant grants and publications concerning AIEd. Second, it helps
educators and scholars identify the active researchers and institutions in
the research on AIEd. Third, researchers and educators are able to
identify relevant journals dealing with AIEd to ﬁnd channels to make
contributions to the community. Fourth, it enables educators and
scholars to be more aware of the top keywords being used in AIEd
studies, which is especially helpful when they are making decisions about
which types of issues to deal with. In addition, this research helps edu-
cators and scholars identify relevant software tools concerning the
application of AI for educational purposes.

the whole ﬁeld of AIEd was undergoing dynamic development. CAEAI
aims to offer a platform for scholars, practitioners, and educators to
publish their studies, share their ideas, and highlight novel systems and
pedagogical innovation on issues concerning AI’s use for educational
purposes. As a sister journal to Computers & Education, CAEAI specif-

data analysis based on AI algorithms, e-learning system design based on
